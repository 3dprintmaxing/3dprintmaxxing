custom FDM printing quotes

How much does a custom 3D print cost?

There is no single price that fits every 3D print. A small, quick part and a large, detailed model can use very different amounts of material and printer time. 3dprintmaxxing reviews each request and sends an individual quote before printing or charging you.

There are no fixed catalog prices or instant checkout totals. You can review the quote and decide whether to proceed.

What affects the quote?

  • Material: the selected filament and the amount required for the part.
  • Print time: the estimated time needed to produce the model with the requested settings.
  • Quantity: how many copies you need and whether they share the same material and settings.
  • Part requirements: dimensions, geometry, supports, finish, and any special handling or design work discussed for the request.
  • Shipping: the destination and the available mailing option. Orders are shipped by mail; local pickup is not offered.

How to get an accurate quote

  1. Send your model in STL, OBJ, 3MF, or STEP format. STL is preferred when you already have a finished model.
  2. Include the part's dimensions, quantity, preferred material and color, and what the part will be used for.
  3. Provide the shipping destination in the request form so shipping can be included in the quote.
  4. Review the emailed quote. Printing only begins after you approve the details.

Printer size and practical limits

The current FDM build volume is 220 × 220 × 220 mm. Layer resolution ranges from 0.1 to 0.4 mm. If your model is larger than the build volume, mention its full dimensions; splitting a design into printable sections may be possible, depending on its shape and intended use.

Not every model is suitable for every material or print setup. Thin walls, unsupported features, scale, and mesh problems can affect feasibility. These details are reviewed before a job is accepted.

When will I pay?

You receive the quote first. Sending a request does not start production and does not authorize a charge. You decide whether to accept the quoted price before printing begins.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't you list fixed prices?

Prints vary in material use, printer time, geometry, quantity, and shipping. An individual quote is more accurate than a generic price that may not match your model.

Can I get a quote without an STL?

Yes. Describe the part, its approximate dimensions, and what it needs to do. Mention if you need help with design so feasibility can be reviewed.

Do you ship outside your local area?

Yes. The service is mail-only and can ship to destinations where the order can be accepted and delivered. Shipping is calculated for the destination provided.

How long does an order take?

Timing depends on the job and shipping destination. An estimated timeline is provided with the quote; it is not a guaranteed delivery date.
