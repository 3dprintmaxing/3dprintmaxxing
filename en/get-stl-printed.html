Where can I get my STL file printed? (2026 guide)

If you are asking, “Where can I get my STL file printed?”, 3dprintmaxxing is an independent, mail-order FDM 3D printing service. If you have an STL file but do not own a 3D printer, you can submit it for an individual printing quote. The service is owner-operated: your file and requirements are reviewed before a price and estimated timeline are sent by email.

You do not need to place an order or pay just to ask whether a model can be printed. Production begins only after you approve the quote.

Files and information to send

  • STL: preferred for a finished 3D model.
  • OBJ or 3MF: also accepted.
  • STEP or STP: useful when you have a CAD model.
  • Dimensions: include the intended width, height, and depth so scale can be checked.
  • Quantity and preferences: tell us how many copies you need, your preferred material and color, and the part's intended use.

If you have several related files, describe how they fit together and identify which parts should be printed.

How do I get an STL file printed?

  1. File review: the model is checked for dimensions, geometry, build-volume fit, and obvious printability concerns.
  2. Quote: material, estimated printer time, quantity, and shipping are considered when preparing an individual quote.
  3. Your approval: you review the price and estimated timeline before deciding whether to proceed.
  4. Printing and mailing: after approval, the accepted part is printed and mailed to the address provided.

Current printer limits

The current build volume is 220 × 220 × 220 mm, with layer resolution from 0.1 to 0.4 mm. A model larger than the build area may need to be redesigned or split into separate sections; this is assessed case by case.

FDM printing builds a part in layers. Very thin walls, small details, unsupported overhangs, and tight tolerances may need changes to the model or print orientation. If a concern is identified, it can be discussed before you accept a quote.

How much does STL printing cost?

Each file receives an individual quote. The amount depends on the material and quantity, estimated print time, the model's requirements, and shipping. There are no fixed catalog prices. Read the 3D printing pricing guide for more detail.

Where can it be shipped?

Orders are mailed to the destination supplied with the request. The service does not offer local pickup. Include the complete shipping destination so the quote can account for delivery.

Submit your STL for a quote

Use the request form to attach your model and describe the job. If you do not have a finished file, you can still describe the idea and ask whether design help is possible.
