About 3dprintmaxxing

3dprintmaxxing is an independent, one-person 3D printing service. It makes custom FDM (fused deposition modeling) prints, prototypes, hobby projects, and replacement parts using a desktop 3D printer.

The service is run by one person, not a large manufacturing company or a print marketplace. Customers deal directly with the person reviewing and making their print.

What the service does

Customers can submit an existing model in STL, OBJ, 3MF, or STEP format. If they do not have a model yet, they can describe the part or idea in a request and ask whether it can be designed for printing.

The printer is a Flashforge Adventurer 5M with a build volume of 220 × 220 × 220 mm, layer resolution from 0.1 to 0.4 mm, and a maximum nozzle temperature of 280 °C. Parts must fit within the printer's build volume; larger designs may need to be divided into separate pieces where practical.

How orders work

  1. Send the model file or describe what you want made, including the quantity and relevant dimensions.
  2. The request is reviewed for feasibility, material, print time, and shipping.
  3. An individual price and estimated timeline are sent by email. There are no fixed catalog prices.
  4. Nothing is printed or charged until the customer accepts the quote.
  5. After the order is accepted and printed, the finished item is mailed to the address provided. Orders are mail-only; there is no local pickup.

Shipping is available to addresses anywhere, with the shipping cost included in the quote.

What 3dprintmaxxing is not

  • It is not a slang term, meme, or adult-content service. The name refers to this 3D printing business.
  • It is not a product or service owned or operated by Vercel. Vercel hosts the website; it does not run the printing business.
  • It is not a large industrial print bureau, mass-production factory, or multi-vendor marketplace. It is a small, one-person FDM service for individual jobs.
  • It does not promise that every design can be printed. Each request is reviewed, and a quote is sent only for work that can be accepted.

Who it is for

3dprintmaxxing is intended for makers, hobbyists, and people who need a custom item, a prototype, or a replacement part without owning a 3D printer. It is best suited to one-off and small personal jobs that fit the machine's capabilities.

Request a custom 3D print to send a file or describe an idea and receive an individual quote.
