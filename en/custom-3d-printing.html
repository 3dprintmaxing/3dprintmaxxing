Custom 3D printing for one-off parts and prototypes

3dprintmaxxing is an independent, owner-operated FDM 3D printing service for custom parts, prototypes, replacement pieces, and hobby projects. If you need a single item or a small quantity, send a model file or describe what you want made and request an individual quote.

Every request is reviewed before production. You receive the price and an estimated timeline by email, including shipping, and decide whether to proceed. Nothing is printed or charged until you approve the quote.

What can I request?

  • One-off custom parts: brackets, covers, adapters, organizers, enclosures, and other non-critical parts, subject to model review.
  • Prototypes: early physical versions that help you check shape, size, assembly, or appearance before revising a design.
  • Replacement pieces: a replacement for an unavailable or broken item may be considered when you can provide a model and useful dimensions. Fit and material suitability must be reviewed.
  • Hobby and display projects: custom objects, models, accessories, and small project parts.

FDM printing is not suitable for every design or application. Do not assume a printed part is appropriate for safety-critical, medical, or other regulated use; explain the intended application so feasibility can be assessed.

Files, materials, and printer size

STL is preferred for a finished model. OBJ, 3MF, and STEP files are also accepted. If you do not have a finished file, describe the idea and ask whether design assistance is possible for that request.

  • Process: FDM (fused deposition modeling) filament printing.
  • Build volume: 220 × 220 × 220 mm.
  • Layer resolution: 0.1–0.4 mm.
  • Material setup: primarily configured for PLA. Ask about another material before assuming it is available.

Thin walls, small details, unsupported overhangs, mesh errors, and tight tolerances can affect whether a model is printable. The file and requirements are reviewed before a quote is finalized.

How the custom 3D printing process works

  1. Send the request: attach your STL, OBJ, 3MF, or STEP file, or describe the item you need.
  2. Include the details: provide dimensions, quantity, preferred material and color, intended use, and shipping destination.
  3. Review the quote: receive an individual price and estimated timeline by email. You can ask questions before deciding.
  4. Approve production: printing begins only after you accept the quote.
  5. Receive the part: completed orders are mailed to the destination provided. Local pickup is not offered.

What affects the price?

Quotes depend on the material and amount used, estimated printer time, quantity, model requirements, and shipping destination. There are no fixed catalog prices because each model is different. Read the custom 3D printing pricing guide for the quote process.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order just one custom 3D printed part?

Yes. One-off requests are welcome, subject to file review and whether the part is suitable for the available FDM setup.

Do I need an STL file?

No. STL is preferred, but OBJ, 3MF, and STEP are also accepted. If you only have an idea or description, send the details and ask whether design assistance is possible.

Can you print a replacement part?

A replacement piece may be considered when its model, dimensions, intended use, and material requirements can be reviewed. A printed part should not be assumed suitable for safety-critical use.

Where do you ship?

Orders are mailed to the destination supplied with the request. Local pickup is not offered; shipping is included in the individual quote.

How long does a custom 3D print take?

The estimated timeline depends on the model, print time, order details, and shipping. An estimate is provided with the quote; it is not a guaranteed delivery date.
