custom FDM 3D printing

Turn an STL file or a rough idea into a physical 3D print.

3dprintmaxxing is an owner-operated FDM 3D printing service for prototypes, replacement parts, hobby projects, cosplay pieces, and small batches. Send the model, describe the result you need, and receive a clear quote before anything is printed or charged.

This is a practical alternative to a large print farm when you want a human to review the geometry, material, scale, and print-time details instead of sending a file into an opaque checkout flow.

What you get

  • Human-reviewed quotes: the model, quantity, material, color, and shipping details are reviewed together.
  • Clear FDM pricing: the quote reflects the requested part and its print parameters rather than a hidden subscription or mandatory account.
  • Useful file support: STL is preferred; OBJ, 3MF, STEP, STP, G-code, and ZIP files can also be included when they help explain the job.
  • Honest feasibility checks: thin walls, unsupported overhangs, scale problems, and STL geometry issues are identified before production when possible.

Materials and printer limits

The primary machine is a Flashforge Adventurer 5M with a 220 × 220 × 220 mm build volume, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.1–0.4 mm layer resolution, and a maximum nozzle temperature of 280 °C. PLA is the main material; other materials can be discussed when the part and printer setup support them.

For material selection, compare the 3D printing filament guide. For print-quality problems, see the first-layer troubleshooting guide and the reliable PLA workflow.

Good fits for this service

Prototypes

Check size, fit, ergonomics, and appearance before committing to a larger production method.

Functional hobby parts

Request brackets, organizers, adapters, enclosures, replacement pieces, and workshop projects within the printer’s limits.

Small batches

Ask about several copies of the same model or a small set of related models with shared requirements.

How the quote works

  1. Send the STL file or describe the idea, including dimensions and intended use.
  2. Receive questions or a quote based on material, quantity, estimated print time, and shipping.
  3. Approve the details before printing begins.
  4. The finished part is printed, checked, and mailed to the address provided.

Request a custom 3D print quote

Use the custom print request form to send your file, dimensions, preferred material, quantity, color, budget, and shipping address. You do not need an STL file if you need help determining whether the idea can be designed and printed.

Pricing is confirmed before charging you. Requests for weapons, illegal items, or explicit items are not accepted.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an STL file?

No. STL is best when you already have a finished model, but you can describe an idea and select that you need design help in the request form.

Can you print large parts?

The current build volume is 220 × 220 × 220 mm. Larger objects may need to be split into printable sections, so include the target dimensions with your request.

Can you print several copies?

Yes. Include the quantity and whether every copy should use the same material and color.

When do I pay?

You receive a quote before printing or charging. The request form is for review and does not by itself start production.
